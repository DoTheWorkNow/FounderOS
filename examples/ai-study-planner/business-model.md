# Business Model: AI Study Planner

## Product or Idea

AI Study Planner.

## Target Customer

Adult exam candidates preparing over a 3 to 12 month period.

## Buyer

The learner.

## Pricing Hypothesis

Freemium for a first plan, then paid weekly replanning and accountability features.

## Revenue Model

Subscription or one-time exam season package.

## Acquisition Channels

- Exam preparation communities
- Study influencers
- SEO pages for specific exam plans
- Free plan generator
- Partnerships with tutors or small course providers

## Activation Moment

The user enters their exam date and availability, then receives a plan that feels more realistic than what they made alone.

## Retention Driver

Weekly replanning, progress review, and recovery after missed days.

## Cost Structure

- AI generation cost
- Product hosting
- Expert review for exam-specific planning templates
- Content and distribution

## Unit Economics Assumptions

| Metric | Assumption | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAC | Low if SEO/community works | Not validated |
| Conversion rate | Unknown | Not validated |
| Gross margin | High if AI cost is controlled | Not validated |
| Payback period | Short for seasonal package | Not validated |

## Risks

- Users may only need one free plan.
- Planning quality may depend heavily on exam domain expertise.
- Motivation and accountability may matter more than planning.
- Course providers could add similar features.

## Validation Plan

Test willingness to use and pay through concierge planning before building software.
