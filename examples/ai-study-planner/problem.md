# Problem: Exam Candidates Cannot Keep Study Plans Realistic

## User

Adult learners preparing for competitive exams while balancing work, school, or family responsibilities.

## Scenario

The user starts with a large syllabus and a fixed exam date. They make an optimistic plan, miss several days, and then no longer know how to adjust.

## Pain Description

The plan becomes emotionally heavy. The user avoids looking at it because it reminds them that they are behind.

## Current Workaround

- Restart from scratch
- Ask friends for schedules
- Follow a course plan even when it no longer fits
- Track progress manually in spreadsheets
- Study whatever feels urgent today

## Why Current Solutions Are Not Good Enough

Most tools assume the plan remains valid. The painful part is not making the first plan, but recovering when reality breaks it.

## Frequency

Weekly, especially after missed study days or mock exam results.

## Intensity

High for serious candidates because the exam date is fixed and falling behind creates anxiety.

## Willingness To Pay

Unknown. Needs validation. Candidates already pay for courses, books, and coaching, but may not pay for planning alone.

## Evidence

No direct interviews yet.

## Related Ideas

- AI Study Planner
- Weekly accountability assistant
- Mock exam recovery planner

## Open Questions

- Is planning the core pain or just a symptom?
- Would users trust AI-generated adjustments?
- Which exams have the clearest repeatable planning structure?
