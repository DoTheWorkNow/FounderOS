# Experiment: Concierge Study Plan Test

## Hypothesis

Exam candidates who feel behind will use a personalized weekly recovery plan and find it valuable enough to request another one.

## Target User

Adult exam candidates within 3 to 6 months of an exam date who have already fallen behind their original plan.

## Experiment Method

Interview 10 users. For 3 of them, manually create a 7-day recovery plan based on their schedule, weak subjects, and recent progress. Follow up after one week.

## Success Criteria

- At least 7 of 10 interviewees describe replanning as a recurring pain.
- At least 2 of 3 concierge users follow the plan for 4 or more days.
- At least 1 of 3 asks for another weekly plan or says they would pay for continued help.

## Time Box

7 days for interviews, then 7 days for follow-up.
